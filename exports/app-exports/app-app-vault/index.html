What it is

AppVault is a marketplace front-end for discovering, buying, and selling apps. It ships a public storefront (browse, categories, app detail, cart, checkout, wishlist) alongside seller tooling and an admin console, built with React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind and the shadcn/ui component system.
